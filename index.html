Chapter one / The journey

From Ranchi to the world.

A timeline of defining milestones. Explore the moments that shaped the legend.

2004 · INTERNATIONAL DEBUT

The journey begins

Made his ODI debut for India against Bangladesh, beginning an international career that would redefine wicketkeeping and finishing.

2007 · CAPTAINCY & WORLD TITLE

A new leader emerges

Led India to the inaugural ICC Men's T20 World Cup title in South Africa.

2011 · THE WORLD CUP

That unforgettable night

India won the ICC Cricket World Cup. Dhoni's unbeaten 91 in the final became one of the defining innings in Indian cricket.

2013 · CHAMPIONS TROPHY

Completing the set

Captained India to the ICC Champions Trophy, becoming the first captain to win all three major ICC limited-overs trophies.

2016 · THE FINISHER

Still delivering

Continued to produce memorable finishes and wicketkeeping performances across formats.

2020 · INTERNATIONAL FAREWELL

An era, forever

Announced his retirement from international cricket, leaving a legacy of leadership, composure and extraordinary moments.
